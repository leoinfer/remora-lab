# MARC-Symbiote

**Status:** `PROPOSED`

MARC-Symbiote treats a compact controller as a semantic and hardware-aware
fingerprint that can attach to a temporary execution body. Semantic
fingerprints describe model/state behavior; hardware fingerprints describe
available compute, memory, and transfer behavior. The research question is
whether the controller can select a measured phenotype without turning the
temporary body into an opaque foreign backend.

The related registry records are N04 (semantic fingerprints), N05 (hardware
fingerprints), and N09 (temporary execution body). The broader MARC-X,
MARC-OS, and MARC-Synapse names are catalogued in the atlas and registry.
This remains a design hypothesis: safety, portability, provenance, and
fallback behavior require explicit certificates and adversarial evaluation.
