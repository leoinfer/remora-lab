# Progressive Future Materialization (PFM)

**Status:** `DEFERRED`

PFM is a speculative-state design in which future-token representations are
materialized progressively instead of committing to one fixed prediction
depth. PFM-A emphasizes elastic/branching future state; PFM-B emphasizes
resource-aware materialization and verification. Both are linked to TBEH,
PHASE, RSSO, and the accepted-token objective.

The exact source is [`PFM_FORMAL_SPECIFICATION.md`](../archival/vault/PFM_FORMAL_SPECIFICATION.md),
with the related architecture in [`Progressive-Future-Materialization.md`](../archival/vault/Progressive-Future-Materialization.md).
The line is deferred because model-shaped tensor contracts, branch-quality
measurements, and end-to-end cost accounting are not yet complete.
