# Accepted-token economics

**Status:** `PROPOSED`

Accepted-token economics treats accepted output, verification, draft work,
bytes transferred, launch overhead, and recovery cost as one objective. It is
the accounting layer shared by speculative decoding, residency, quantization,
and adaptive maintenance. A higher draft acceptance rate is not sufficient if
the rejected or transferred work erases the gain.

The source equations and falsifiers are distributed through the HERMES atlas,
REMORA master prompt, TBEH, and the accepted-token roofline checker F1. See
[`REMORA`](../../systems/REMORA.md), [`DSpark / MTP`](../../systems/DSpark-MTP.md),
and [`F1`](../../theory/checkers/F1-checker.md).
