# Waste Ledger

**Status:** `PROPOSED`

The Waste Ledger records rejected draft work, unused prefetched state,
stranded bytes, avoidable conversions, synchronization stalls, and failed
recovery attempts. It is intended to make hidden costs visible in accepted-
token and residency comparisons.

Source: the REMORA master prompt and HERMES atlas. Related records are
[`ACCEPTED-TOKEN-ECONOMICS.md`](ACCEPTED-TOKEN-ECONOMICS.md),
[`F10`](../../theory/checkers/F10-checker.md), and the negative knowledge
ledger.
