# Expert Execution Residency (EER)

**Status:** `PROPOSED`

EER is the family of ideas around expert-aware residency: stable slots,
expert capsules, predictive prefetch, hot/warm/cold state, frozen epochs,
dual/tri-path joins, and cost-triggered repacking. The target metric is value
per resident byte and accepted work, not cache hit rate in isolation.

Read [`ExpertPack`](../../systems/ExpertPack.md),
[`REMORA`](../../systems/REMORA.md), and the formal/prototype material in
[`archival/vault`](../../archival/vault/). Model expert payloads and raw
residency traces are not part of this candidate.
