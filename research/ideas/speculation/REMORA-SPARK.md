# REMORA-Spark

**Status:** `PROPOSED`

REMORA-Spark groups elastic future-token prediction with resource-aware
verification. It connects TBEH's horizon choice, PHASE's outcome tree,
PFM's progressive materialization, RSSO's authority boundary, and the
accepted-token objective. The design must account for rejected work, branch
state, transfers, and tail risk rather than optimizing draft tokens alone.

See [`DSpark / MTP`](../../systems/DSpark-MTP.md),
[`TBEH`](TBEH.md), [`PHASE`](PHASE.md), and the exact source documents under
[`archival/vault`](../../archival/vault/).
