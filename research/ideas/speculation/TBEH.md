# TBEH

**Status:** `PROPOSED`

Tail-Bounded Elastic Horizon chooses speculative depth using predicted
acceptance, verification cost, and a bound on harmful tail behavior. The
intended policy is adaptive: a long horizon is valuable only when expected
accepted work exceeds its draft, transfer, and recovery costs.
