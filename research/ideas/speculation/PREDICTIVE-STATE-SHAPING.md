# Predictive state shaping

**Status:** `PROPOSED`

Predictive state shaping asks whether future-token or route predictions can
shape the state layout before the final authority decision, reducing exposed
bytes and synchronization without treating a prediction as proof.

This is a registry-level research record connected to PFM, RSSO, PHASE,
expert-major execution, and the dependency/state-boundary conjectures. The
source and uncertainty labels remain in
[`IDEA_REGISTRY.jsonl`](../../archival/vault/IDEA_REGISTRY.jsonl).
