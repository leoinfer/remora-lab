# Research roadmap

The roadmap separates runtime completion from research validation. A research
idea may remain useful while its implementation is deferred, its evidence is
incomplete, or a counterexample blocks a broad claim.

See [`HAR_ROADMAP.md`](HAR_ROADMAP.md), the experiment queue, and the open
problems. Publication of this expanded candidate remains blocked until the
larger-tree audits are rerun and reviewed.
