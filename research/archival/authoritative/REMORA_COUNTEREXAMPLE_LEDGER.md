## Ledger use rules

1. Every counterexample carries its scope; no broader falsification is inferred.
2. A failed performance result with a correctness failure is retained as failure evidence, not averaged into a baseline.
3. A static/simulated counterexample cannot become a hardware claim.
4. A counterexample found by the theory checker should be added to the source Failure Ledger only by the experimental owner; this discovery pass does not modify that ledger.
5. The decisive machine-checkable artifacts for these entries are queued in `REMORA_FORMALIZATION_QUEUE.md`.
