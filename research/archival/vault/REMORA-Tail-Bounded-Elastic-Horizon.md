# REMORA Tail-Bounded Elastic Horizon (TBEH)

TBEH is a cross-cutting controller mechanism for Batch 1 Ideas 1, 2 and 5,
Batch 3 REMORA Portion, and Batch 6 Delta-Certified skipping. It translates a
general mathematical proof strategy—explicit dominant contributions plus a
controlled omitted remainder—into an inference horizon design. It does not
claim that literal gamma-function or complex-analysis equations describe
transformer inference.

For position `k`, estimate conditional acceptance `c[t,k]`, cumulative survival
`a[t,k]`, retained value `R_t(L)`, omitted tail `T_t(L)`, and incremental cost
`C[t,k]`. Select the smallest integer horizon whose conservative upper tail
value is below extension cost plus memory, rollback and contention opportunity
cost. A geometric survival bound using `rho < 1` is allowed only when held-out
assumptions are validated; otherwise use empirical quantile or conformal upper
bounds.

Every rare expensive event records both absolute and relative error. PHASE
branch preparation uses omitted probability mass times maximum avoidable future
cost versus branch, memory, validation, contention and eviction cost. REMORA
Portion may later include tail-risk buffer and uncertainty surplus, but the
policy equation is not established.

**Current evidence:** theoretical/offline only. The historical unfixed dense B0
negative control failed, but the isolated production-fix build now has a frozen
10-run exact baseline and native-MTP accepted-token observations. Those runs are
not held-out target-authoritative TBEH traces, and no calibration/evaluation
separation or tail-bound proof exists. The design artifacts explicitly use
`NOT_RUN`, not zero-filled measurements. Full target verification remains
authoritative, and the fail-closed fallback is fixed safe depth/full target.
