# Falsified and negative knowledge

This directory records rejected claims, counterexamples, negative experiments,
and missing-evidence boundaries. A falsified card is valuable research state:
it narrows the safe design space and prevents a historical result from being
promoted to a general claim.

Start with [`counterexamples/`](counterexamples/),
[`V4_NEGATIVE_KNOWLEDGE.md`](V4_NEGATIVE_KNOWLEDGE.md), and the preserved
failure ledgers. The dedicated [`gfx1200 sparse-matrix anomaly`](GFX1200_SPARSE_MATRIX_ANOMALY.md)
shows how an exciting result was killed by stronger known-answer accounting. The
2026-09-19 → 2026-09-22 campaign's own rejections and retractions are in
[`ALICE_CAMPAIGN_NEGATIVES.md`](ALICE_CAMPAIGN_NEGATIVES.md): the F2 copy-stream
null, the CPU-MoE prefill regression, the memory-infeasible double staging, the
reverted `ALICE_MOE_BLOCK` lane, the closed 100k raw-decode and structured-
sparsity directions, the Laya sidecar, and the internal retractions. Raw logs,
private receipts, model payloads, and machine identifiers are intentionally
absent.
