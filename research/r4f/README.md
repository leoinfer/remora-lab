# R4F research

R4F is the experimental native Flash-Next-oriented container and execution
direction inside HAR. The research identified container, state, and recovery
boundaries but did not complete a native full-model generation path.

Keep this area separate from two other things that are easy to conflate with it:
the deployment lane that currently generates Flash-Next coherently (an external
llama.cpp-derived research runtime, not part of HAR), and stable HAR runtime
claims. The dated records are
[`research/flash-next/CURRENT_RESEARCH_LOG.md`](../flash-next/CURRENT_RESEARCH_LOG.md)
and [`research/flash-next/CURRENT_CAMPAIGN.md`](../flash-next/CURRENT_CAMPAIGN.md).
