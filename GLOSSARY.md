# Glossary

- **HAR** — Hardware-Aware Runtime, the Rust production runtime in `har/`.
- **R4X** — An experimental weight/block representation used by one research
  lane; its D32A profile is not presented as a standard.
- **R4F** — The experimental Flash-Next bring-up/container direction.
- **R4KV** — Experimental compressed key/value cache profiles and pages.
- **Effective context** — Usable information budget after representation,
  retrieval, quality, and recovery constraints; not merely a position count.
- **MTP** — Multi-token prediction or speculative draft/acceptance control,
  depending on the experiment.
- **Residency** — The state and accounting for model or expert data across
  storage, host memory, and device memory.
- **Reference** — A native Rust correctness oracle or a recorded external
  comparison, never a hidden execution fallback.
- **Receipt** — A reproducible measurement record with provenance, command,
  context, and raw output.
