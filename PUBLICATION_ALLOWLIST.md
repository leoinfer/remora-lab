# Publication allowlist

The release candidate may contain:

- Rust source and Cargo manifests required to build HAR.
- Rust source for the dependency-free publication audit.
- Reviewed SPIR-V-compatible shader source and small synthetic or redacted
  binary fixtures.
- Fresh specifications, research summaries, claims, methodology, and
  provenance records.
- Complete license texts and third-party notices for included material.

Each included file must have known provenance, an applicable license, no
private paths or identifiers, and a destination in the public inventory.

The allowlist does not authorize model weights, checkpoints, tokenizer dumps,
raw datasets, raw receipts, screenshots, local machine telemetry, or copied
upstream implementations.
